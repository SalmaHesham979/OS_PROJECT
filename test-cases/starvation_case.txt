============================================================
         TEST SCENARIO: STARVATION (LONG PROCESS DELAY)
============================================================
Description: This scenario tests how a low-priority process (P1) is "starved" by a stream of high-priority, short processes.

INPUT DATA:
| Process | Arrival Time | Burst Time | Priority |
|---------|--------------|------------|----------|
| P1      | 0            | 20         | 10       |
| P2      | 1            | 3          | 1        |
| P3      | 3            | 3          | 1        |
| P4      | 5            | 3          | 1        |
| P5      | 7            | 3          | 1        |

------------------------------------------------------------
1. PRIORITY PREEMPTIVE RESULTS
------------------------------------------------------------
Gantt Chart:
| P1 | P2 | P3 | P4 | P5 |         P1         |
0    1    4    7    10   13                  32

Output Metrics:
| Process | WT | TAT | RT |
|---------|----|-----|----|
| P1      | 12 | 32  | 0  |
| P2      | 0  | 3   | 0  |
| P3      | 1  | 4   | 1  |
| P4      | 2  | 5   | 2  |
| P5      | 3  | 6   | 3  |
|---------|----|-----|----|
| AVERAGE | 3.60| 10.00| 1.20 |

------------------------------------------------------------
2. SRTF RESULTS
------------------------------------------------------------
Gantt Chart:
| P1 | P2 | P3 | P4 | P5 |         P1         |
0    1    4    7    10   13                  32

Output Metrics:
| Process | WT | TAT | RT |
|---------|----|-----|----|
| P1      | 12 | 32  | 0  |
| P2      | 0  | 3   | 0  |
| P3      | 1  | 4   | 1  |
| P4      | 2  | 5   | 2  |
| P5      | 3  | 6   | 3  |
|---------|----|-----|----|
| AVERAGE | 3.60| 10.00| 1.20 |

KEY OBSERVATIONS:
1. Starvation Event: P1 starts at T=0 but is immediately kicked out at T=1 because a much higher priority process (P2) arrives.
2. Continuous Delay: Because P3, P4, and P5 keep arriving with high priority, P1 is forced to wait in the ready queue for a long time (Starvation).
3. Metric Impact: P1's waiting time is very high compared to others.

CONCLUSION:
In both algorithms, P1 suffers from starvation. This workload demonstrates the need for "Aging" in real-world operating systems to eventually increase the priority of long-waiting tasks.
