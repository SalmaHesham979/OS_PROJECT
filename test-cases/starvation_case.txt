============================================================
        TEST SCENARIO: STARVATION (LONG PROCESS DELAY)
============================================================
Description: This scenario is specifically designed to test how a long, low-priority process (P1) can be "starved" of CPU time
by a continuous stream of shorter, higher-priority processes.

INPUT DATA:
| Process | Arrival Time | Burst Time | Priority |
|---------|--------------|------------|----------|
| P1      | 0            | 15         | 10       |
| P2      | 1            | 2          | 1        |
| P3      | 2            | 2          | 1        |
| P4      | 3            | 2          | 1        |

------------------------------------------------------------
1. PRIORITY PREEMPTIVE RESULTS
------------------------------------------------------------
Gantt Chart:
| P1 | P2 | P3 | P4 |               P1               |
0    1    3    5    7                               21

Output Metrics:
| Process | WT | TAT | RT |
|---------|----|-----|----|
| P1      | 6  | 21  | 0  |
| P2      | 0  | 2   | 0  |
| P3      | 1  | 3   | 1  |
| P4      | 2  | 4   | 2  |
|---------|----|-----|----|
| AVERAGE | 2.25| 7.50| 0.75 |

------------------------------------------------------------
2. SRTF RESULTS
------------------------------------------------------------
Gantt Chart:
| P1 | P2 | P3 | P4 |               P1               |
0    1    3    5    7                               21

Output Metrics:
| Process | WT | TAT | RT |
|---------|----|-----|----|
| P1      | 6  | 21  | 0  |
| P2      | 0  | 2   | 0  |
| P3      | 1  | 3   | 1  |
| P4      | 2  | 4   | 2  |
|---------|----|-----|----|
| AVERAGE | 2.25| 7.50| 0.75 |

KEY OBSERVATIONS:
1. Preemption Event: At T=1, Process P2 arrives. P1 is immediately preempted because P2 has a much higher priority (1 vs 10).
2. The Starvation Chain: P1 stays in the "Ready Queue" from T=1 until T=7. It "starves" because the scheduler keeps favoring newer, more
   "important" and "shorter" processes (P2, P3, P4).
3. Identical Behavior: Both algorithms produced identical results here because the shortest jobs also had the highest priority levels.

CONCLUSION:
This case proves that without an "Aging" mechanism, low-priority tasks like P1 will always suffer from significant delays (Starvation)
in preemptive systems like Priority and SRTF.
